慢性疼痛長期阿片類治療患者使用醫用大麻與阿片類藥物處方中止之關聯

Journal of addiction medicine · Medical Cannabis and Opioid Prescription Discontinuation Among Chronic Pain Patients on Long-term Opioid Therapy.
